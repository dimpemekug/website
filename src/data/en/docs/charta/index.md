Charta is a Markdown editor for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It works on plain `.md` text files: no database and no format of its own. When you open a file, Charta only changes the characters you type; the rest of the file stays as it is.

## Two ways of writing the same file

- **WYSIWYG**: you see your text already formatted. Markdown marks (`**`, `#`, `[…](…)`) show up only where the cursor is and disappear when you move on. Tables, math, diagrams and images are drawn in the text.
- **Code editor**: the Markdown itself, with syntax colors and line numbers, and the preview beside or below it.

Switch between the two with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> or with the button in the window's toolbar. The mode documents open in is set in **Settings › General › Open documents in**. Details in [WYSIWYG and code editor](/en/projects/charta/docs/wysiwyg-and-code-editor).

## Single files and folders

- **A file**: on the Mac with **File › Open…** or from Finder; on iPhone and iPad from the Files app or from Charta's launch screen.
- **A folder**: with **File › Open Folder…** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>) on the Mac, or **Open Folder** on the launch screen on iPhone and iPad. The folder becomes a project, with a note tree, search, `[[note]]` links and the graph. See [Folders and linked notes](/en/projects/charta/docs/folders-and-linked-notes).

In folders, notes save themselves shortly after each change. On the Mac, the save status appears under the window title (for example "Saved 2 min ago").

## Requirements

Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later; iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 26 or later. Charta is free, with no ads or in-app purchases, needs no account and collects no data. The interface is in English and Italian: choose the language in **Settings › General**.
