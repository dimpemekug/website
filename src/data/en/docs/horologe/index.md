## What is Horologe

**Horologe** is a native mobile application for **iOS** and **Android** designed for watch enthusiasts and horology collectors who want to document their timepieces with encyclopedic precision, preserving high-resolution photos, movement specifications, service histories, wrist time logs, and warranty records.

Constructed using premier native technologies (**SwiftUI** on iOS and **Jetpack Compose** on Android), Horologe pairs the refined visual aesthetics of fine watchmaking with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture.

---

## Core Strengths

### 1. Detailed Horological Cataloging
Document every timepiece down to subtle technical nuances:
- Brand, model, nickname, reference, and serial number.
- Movement architecture (Manual Wind, Automatic, Quartz, Spring Drive, Solar) and caliber reference.
- Case dimensions (diameter, thickness, lug-to-lug, lug width) and materials (316L/904L steel, titanium, gold, bronze, ceramic).
- Crystal, bezel, dial details, caseback engravings, water resistance rating, and fitted straps.
- Acquisition data (purchase price, date, vendor, box and papers status).

### 2. Multi-Photo Gallery & Document Archive
Attach dedicated high-resolution shots for each piece (cover shot, dial close-up, case flank, caseback, exposed movement, buckle/strap) alongside receipts, appraisal certificates, and warranty cards in PDF or image format.

### 3. Maintenance Records & Service Reminders
Log technical maintenance intervals (ultrasonic cleaning, complete movement overhaul, gasket renewal, pressure testing), recording service costs, dates, watchmaker details, and warranty terms.

### 4. Wrist Time & Chronometric Accuracy
- **Accuracy Log**: record timing accuracy deviations (seconds per day) compared against atomic time standards.
- **Wrist Time Tracking**: log with one tap when a watch is worn to monitor collection rotation and usage frequency.
