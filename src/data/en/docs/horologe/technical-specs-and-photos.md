## Creating a Watch Sheet

When you add a watch (the `+` button) you can fill in these fields; only brand and model are required.

### Identification
- **Brand and model**: e.g. *Omega Speedmaster Professional*, *Seiko SKX007*.
- **Reference and serial number**: the manufacturer's official codes.
- **Year** of production.

### Technical Details
- **Movement**: free text (e.g. automatic, hand-wound, quartz).
- **Caliber**: e.g. *3861*, *7S26*.
- **Diameter**: e.g. *39 mm*, *42 mm*.
- **Case material**: e.g. steel, titanium, gold, bronze, ceramic.

### Purchase and Status
- **Price and currency**, **purchase date**.
- **Box** and **papers**: whether the watch comes with its full set.
- **Status**: in the collection, in repair, sold (with sale date and price, to work out the gain) or on the wishlist.
- **Service interval**: how many years between services, for reminders.
- Free-form **notes**.

### Custom Fields
Anything without a dedicated field (crystal, water resistance, power reserve, lug width, straps…) can be added as a **custom field**, with a name and value of your choice.

## Photos and Documents

1. **Cover photo**: the image that represents the watch in your collection. Pick it from your photo library or, on iOS, take it with the camera.
2. **Gallery**: more photos of the dial, bezel, caseback, movement or straps, viewable full screen.
3. **Attachments**: warranties, certificates, invoices and receipts, as images or PDFs, each with a name.

### Safe File Paths
Images are stored in the app's private storage. On both iOS and Android, Horologe finds them again even if the app's folder path changes after a system update, by looking them up by name in the `watch_images` folder.
