## The Collection

The app's first tab shows your watches.

### Summary
At the top, three tiles: the number of watches in the collection, the **value** (one line per currency, if you use more than one) and the **Wishlist**. Tapping the value opens Statistics; tapping the wishlist filters the list. Below them is the **Recently worn** carousel.

### Grid or list
Switch between grid and list with the button at the top; your choice is remembered. On iPad and on wide Android screens the collection is always a grid.

A long press on a watch opens a menu with the most common actions, such as **Worn today**.

### Search and filters
- **Search** looks through every field of the watch.
- The status filters show the number of watches next to each item: **In collection** (the default: owned and in repair), **All**, and one for each status that has at least one watch.
- The **Brand** and **Movement** menus appear when the collection has at least two different values.

### Ten sort orders
1. Recently added (default)
2. First added
3. A-Z
4. Z-A
5. Highest price
6. Lowest price
7. Newest year
8. Oldest year
9. Recently purchased
10. Recently worn

## Statistics

The second tab gathers your collection's numbers, with the same content on iOS and Android (in two columns on iPad).

- **Collection value**: total per currency, number of watches, average value, *full set* pieces (with box and papers) and a chart of how the value grew over the years.
- **Did you know?**: a few insights, such as an overdue service, a watch you haven't worn for at least 30 days, or the cost per wear of your favorite.
- **Value**: your most expensive piece and the sales balance, with the watches you sold.
- **Care**: number of services, how much you spent and upcoming services.
- **Brands** and **Movements**: how the collection is spread; tap an item to open the list of those watches.
- **On the wrist**: the map of the days you wore a watch, the link to the **Wear diary** and the ranking of the **most worn**.

Statistics update by themselves whenever the collection changes.
