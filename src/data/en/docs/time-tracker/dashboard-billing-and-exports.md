## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab turns logged hours into clear information:

- **Headline total**: the hours worked in the chosen period.
- **Hours per project**: charts and percentages showing how work is spread across projects and clients.
- **Billable share**: separates the hours of projects with billing turned on from non-billable ones (e.g. training or internal work).
- **Revenue**: for each billable project, hours multiplied by the hourly rate, VAT included.

---

## Periods and Filters

You can analyze your data by choosing:
- **Quick periods**: the current week, month or year;
- **Custom range**: any start and end dates (e.g. to prepare a quarterly invoice);
- **Project filter**: one or more projects, to see the hours for a single client.

---

## PDF and CSV Export

- **PDF report**: a laid-out summary of the period with total hours, breakdown by project, billable totals and revenue, plus the list of activities; ready to attach to an invoice or send to a client.
- **CSV**: the raw activity data, with date, start and end times, hours in decimal form, project, title, notes and a daily-total flag, to open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

---

## JSON Backup and Restore

In **Settings**:
- **Export backup**: creates a `.json` file with all projects and activities.
- **Import backup**: restores your data on any iOS or Android device, with no conversion.
- **Sample data** and **database reset**, to try the app or start from scratch.
