## Business Analytics Dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab turns logged durations into actionable business intelligence:

- **Hero Summary Metric**: highlights total logged hours over the selected period.
- **Project Allocation**: bar charts and percentage distributions indicating time allocation across clients.
- **Billable Ratio**: distinguishes invoiceable client work from non-billable administrative hours.
- **Financial Projections**: computes gross billable amounts and estimated VAT/sales tax liabilities.

---

## Flexible Time Filters

Examine productivity across tailored windows:
- **Quick Periods**: This Week, This Month, This Year, or comparison views against preceding periods.
- **Custom Date Ranges**: set custom start and end boundaries for specific client billing cycles.
- **Project Filtering**: isolate individual projects to prepare client-specific timesheets.

---

## Professional PDF & CSV Exports

- **Formatted PDF Timesheet**: generates clean A4 summary reports with project breakdowns, daily logs, and tax calculations ready to accompany invoices.
- **CSV Data Export**: download raw data records (timestamps, decimal durations, notes, rates) for custom analysis in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

---

## Universal JSON Backup & Restore

In **Settings › Backup & Restore**:
- **Export Backup**: creates a universal `.json` archive of your complete database.
- **Import Backup**: restores all historical data seamlessly onto any iOS or Android device.
