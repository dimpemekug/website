## Connecting External Repositories

In addition to local archives, rManga supports modular remote sources:

### Community Repositories (Aidoku Standard)
- Add third-party extension repositories using manifest URLs (e.g., `https://.../index.min.json`).
- Quick configuration via deep links: tapping a link with the scheme `rmanga://add-repo?url=...` launches the app and imports the repository automatically.

### Suwayomi & Paperback Servers
- Connect to local or remote self-hosted Suwayomi instances to browse and stream your home comic server securely.

## Offline Reading & Downloads

Prepare for travel or offline reading:
- **Individual or Batch Downloads**: tap the download button beside any chapter or choose "Download all unread chapters".
- **Download Queue Manager**: monitor background download progress from a dedicated transfer panel.
- **Downloaded Filter**: toggle the "Downloaded only" view in your library to access locally cached volumes while disconnected from the internet.
