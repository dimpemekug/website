## Reading Modes

Configure pagination globally or individually per series:

### 1. Right-to-Left (Japanese Manga)
Authentic manga reading: tap the left edge or swipe right to advance to the next page, while tapping the right side flips back.

### 2. Left-to-Right (Western Comics)
Standard western reading mode for graphic novels and comic books advancing from left to right.

### 3. Continuous Vertical Webtoon
Designed for infinite-scroll manhwa and webcomics: pages display seamlessly stacked with zero margins, delivering smooth vertical scrolling.

## Gestures, Zoom, and Controls

- **Center Tap**: reveals the reader overlay (slider bar, chapter selection menu, orientation lock, and display settings).
- **Pinch-to-Zoom & Double Tap**: freely zoom into details with two-finger pinch gestures or double-tap to snap to smart zoom.
- **Crop White Borders**: automatically trims blank margins around page edges to maximize visible art area.
- **Progress Persistence**: closing the reader saves your exact position, allowing you to resume immediately from *Continue Reading*.
