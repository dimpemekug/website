## What is DomoMeter?

**DomoMeter** is a native app for **iPhone and iPad** and for **Android** that helps you keep track of your household usage from meter readings you enter by hand. Electricity, gas and water are ready to go; you can add other utilities, for example solar panels, wood pellets or a hot water meter.

Instead of scattered bills, scraps of paper and mental arithmetic, DomoMeter brings all your utilities together on one screen, with a reading calendar, monthly and yearly charts, an end-of-period forecast and, if you enter your tariffs, a bill estimate.

---

## Principles

### 1. Privacy and offline use
- **No account**: no sign-up, email or password.
- **Your data stays yours**: readings and history live only in the device's storage (on iOS you can turn on sync with your private iCloud). On Android the app doesn't even have permission to access the Internet.
- **Free**: no ads, no tracking, no subscription or in-app purchases.

### 2. Usage split across months
Meters show cumulative values. When you enter two readings, DomoMeter calculates usage as the difference between them. If the readings straddle a change of month, usage is **split in proportion to the days elapsed** in each month: monthly comparisons stay accurate even if you read the meter on irregular dates.

### 3. Time bands (F1, F2, F3)
For each utility you can record a single reading or the values of the three time bands F1, F2 and F3, useful for seeing when you use the most under your tariff. They are usually for electricity, but you can turn them on for any utility.

### 4. Multiple homes
You can create several **home profiles** (home, office, holiday home), each with its own utilities and readings.

## How to use it

1. **Set up your utilities**: on first launch, choose which utilities to track, from electricity, gas, water and the ones you create, with a name, icon and unit (kWh, Smc, litres, cubic metres, kilograms or a simple count). If you like, set a yearly limit and a tariff for each.
2. **Enter the starting reading**: record the meter's current value.
3. **Add further readings**: whenever you like, for example at the end of the month or when the bill arrives. A reminder can prompt you. DomoMeter immediately works out the usage for the period.
4. **Check charts and forecasts**: see how much of the yearly limit you have used, where you will end up by the end of the period and how you compare with last year.

To try the app, you can load **sample data** from Settings.
