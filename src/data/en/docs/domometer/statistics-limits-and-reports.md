## The home screen

DomoMeter's home screen shows the status of every utility in the active profile:

- **Reading calendar**: a year in a grid, with the days you logged readings highlighted. Tap a day to see that day's readings and open a utility's history.
- **Utilities**: for each one, usage for the period, the share of the limit, the total, the last reading and how many days ago you took it; with time bands on, the F1, F2 and F3 usage too.
- **Period**: the current month by default. In the utility editor, under **Period on Home**, you can choose a two-month, six-month or yearly period: the limit and forecast are rescaled to match.
- **Bill estimate** and **usage forecast**, if you have entered your tariffs.

---

## Statistics

At the top of the **Statistics** screen you'll find where each utility is heading by the end of the period, compared with the same period a year earlier, and your estimated spending compared the same way. Below:

- **Monthly usage** for the last 12 months, for each utility, with bars split by time band and the monthly limit line; the month in progress is marked separately.
- **Monthly spending**, worked out with the tariff in force in each month.
- **Year-on-year comparison**, to see seasonal trends (e.g. gas in winter, or electricity in summer with the air conditioning on).
- **Advanced statistics**: daily average for the period, most expensive time band and estimated CO₂ per utility and in total.

## Yearly limits

For each utility you can set an **expected yearly usage**, a reference limit (e.g. 2,700 kWh a year for electricity or 800 Smc for gas):

1. **Setting it**: enter the limit in the utility editor, in the unit you prefer.
2. **Share used**: DomoMeter shows how much of the limit you have already used; the charts also show the matching monthly limit.
3. **Warnings**: when you get close to the threshold or go past it, the utility's bar changes colour.

## Tariffs, bills and budget

If you enter a tariff, DomoMeter also estimates your spending. If you leave it empty, the app shows usage only.

- **Tariff per utility**: price per unit (or a price per band, if time bands are on) and a monthly standing charge.
- **Billing**: monthly, two-monthly or six-monthly. The bill estimate shows the spending so far in the period and the projected amount at its end.
- **Tariff history**: each tariff applies to the months it covered, so switching supplier doesn't rewrite what past months cost.
- **Budget**: a spending cap per billing period, with the share already used.
- **Currency**: in Settings, under **Currency**. *Automatic* follows the device's region; if the one you need isn't listed, type its three-letter code (e.g. `CHF`). Changing currency only changes how amounts are shown: tariffs and budgets already entered are not converted.
- **Estimated CO₂**: by entering the kg of CO₂ per unit in the utility editor, the app shows a rough estimate of emissions.

## Reading reminders

From **Settings › Reminders** you can turn on a reminder for each utility, with:

- the **frequency**: every month, every 2, 3 or 6 months, or once a year;
- the **first month**, the **day** of the month (1 to 28) and the **time**.

The reminder is a local notification, scheduled on the device. If you have already logged that month's reading, the notification doesn't come. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn on the first reminder; if you deny it or turn notifications off in the system settings, the reminders switch off.
