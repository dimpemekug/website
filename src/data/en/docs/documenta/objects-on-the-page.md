## What you can place on the page

From the **Insert** menu:

- **Text Box**;
- **WordArt**: a box with curved text;
- **Shape**: rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, triangle, diamond, hexagon, star, block arrow, callout, line and arrow. You can type inside closed shapes;
- **Image on Page…**: an image placed freely on the page, unlike **Image…** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>), which puts it in the line of text. **Format › Object › Place on Page** moves an in-line image onto the page.

Every object is anchored to a paragraph: it moves with it if **Move with Text** is on, otherwise it stays put on the page.

## Text wrapping

In **Format › Object** and in the Arrange section of the inspector:

- **Around the Object**: text flows around the frame;
- **Tight**: text follows the outline of the shape or the visible part of a transparent image;
- **Top and Bottom**: no text at the sides;
- **In Front of Text** and **Behind Text**.

## Selecting, moving, ordering

- A click selects the object: drag it, resize it with the handles and rotate it. The arrow keys move it.
- <kbd>⇧</kbd>- or <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click selects several objects; **Edit › Select Objects on Page** takes them all.
- **Arrange**: Bring to Front, Bring Forward, Send Backward, Send to Back.
- **Align to Page**: left, center, right, top, middle, bottom.
- **Align Objects** (with two or more objects): aligns edges or centers, or distributes horizontally and vertically.
- **Group** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>G</kbd>) and **Ungroup** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>G</kbd>). Resizing a group scales all its objects.

## Appearance

In the inspector, on the object's tab: fill with color, opacity and gradient, border, size and rotation, and **alternative text** describing the object.

## WordArt

The **Curved Text** section chooses the curve: Stretched, Arch Up, Arch Down, Circle, Wave and Inflate. You type right on the curve. Letter outline and shadow are set in **Effects**, in the Font section. WordArt from Word files, including old Word 97-2007 ones, and LibreOffice Fontwork open as curved boxes.

## Images

- **Crop** and rotation really change the image (a PNG stays PNG); you can add a border and a shadow.
- **Replace Image…** puts another image in place of the selected one.
- **File › Reduce Image Size…** slims the file: see [Formats and compatibility](/en/projects/documenta/docs/formats-and-compatibility).

In RTF, DOC and plain text, text boxes become text and shapes are lost.
