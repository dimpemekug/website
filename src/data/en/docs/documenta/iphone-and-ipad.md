Documenta for iPhone and iPad opens, edits and saves the same documents as the Mac, with the same layout engine. It requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.

## The start screen

When the app opens you see the **New Document** button, the row of **Templates** and, in a sheet you drag up, the system file browser: recents, shared, iCloud Drive and other services. Documents saved by Documenta show a preview of their first page. The browser opens DOCX, ODT, RTF, Markdown, plain text and `.doc`; documents also open from the Files app. The **Documents** button at the top left brings you back to this screen.

- Tapping a template creates a new document from it.
- Touching and holding a template shows its first page large, with **Create**. Personal templates also have **Rename…** and **Delete Template**: deleting a template removes its file, not the documents already created from it.

New documents go in the **Documenta** folder in iCloud Drive or on the device, as Word, OpenDocument or RTF, as you choose in **Settings › New Documents**. Those in the Documenta folder in iCloud Drive show up on the Mac; with **Handoff** a document in iCloud Drive open on one device can be picked up on the other. Saving is automatic.

## Personal templates

**Save as Template…** (in the … menu on iPhone, in the title menu on iPad) saves the open document as a template. Personal templates are files in the **Templates** folder inside the new-documents folder and appear on the start screen after the ten ready-made templates, with a preview of their first page. On the Mac, a personal template is instead a document you choose, wherever you keep it: see [Templates, saving and settings](/en/projects/documenta/docs/templates-saving-and-settings).

## The top bar

To the right of the title are **Undo**, **Redo** (on iPad), the **+** menu for inserting, the **Format** button (on iPhone) or **Inspector** (on iPad), and the **…** menu.

The **+** menu has table, image, text box, shape, footnote, comment and equation at the top, and four submenus below:

- **Objects**: WordArt, Image on Page…, Drawing…, Text from Image…, Scan Document…;
- **Breaks**: page, column, line and section breaks (on a new page or on the same page);
- **Notes and References**: endnote, link, bookmarks, caption, cross-reference, table of figures and table of contents;
- **Fields and Date**: page number, page count, today's date that updates, date and time.

The **…** menu is split into sections:

- document: **Share** (Print… and Export As), **Save as Template…**, **Compare with an Earlier Version…**; on iPhone also **Redo** and **Rename…**, which on iPad are in the title menu;
- search: **Find**, **More Searches** (Find and Replace, Advanced Find) and **Go to Page…**;
- **Review**: **Track Changes**, **Comments** and, when there are tracked changes or open comments, the commands to step through them, accept or reject them all, and **Resolve and Go to Next**;
- **View**: **Pageless**, **Focus Mode**, page appearance and **Read Aloud**;
- **Settings**.

## Writing on iPhone

- On iPhone the text starts **pageless**, as wide as the screen; the **Pageless** item in the … menu switches to real pages, which you zoom with your fingers. Paper, margins and columns stay in the file even without pages.
- The **bar above the keyboard** scrolls sideways and has the **+** menu, the table menu (with the cursor in a table), paragraph style, bold, italic, lists and indents, alignment and underline. **Undo** and the button to hide the keyboard always stay visible on the right.

## The Format sheet

The **Format** sheet (on iPad, the inspector) has the **Style**, **Page** and **Document** tabs, plus **Table** with the cursor in a table and **Object** with an object selected. It reopens on the last tab you used.

- **Style**: font, size and text style, color, effects (character style, capitals, superscript and subscript, highlight, language), alignment and paragraph direction, lists and indents, line spacing, spacing, tabs, borders and background, drop cap and paragraph style.
- **Table**: rows and columns, merged cells, cell background and alignment, style, borders, repeated header and **formulas**, also **On Specific Cells…**; see [Tables](/en/projects/documenta/docs/tables).
- **Object**: text wrap, fill, border, size and rotation, position and order; for text boxes, WordArt's **Curved Text**.
- **Page**: paper, orientation, margins, including **mirror margins** and a **gutter**, and text columns. From here you open **Header and Footer** (one or three columns, different first page and even pages, page number format and starting number) and **Border, Watermark and More** (automatic hyphenation, line numbers, page border, text or image watermark). The settings apply to the section the cursor is in, as on the Mac.
- **Document**: pages, words, characters, words and characters in the selection, reading time, word goal and readability; change tracking with the author's name, and **Number Headings**.

In headers and footers the page number and total are written as `{page}` and `{pages}`; with a single column the **Insert Page Number** menu adds them for you, also as **Page N of M**.

## iPad like a computer

- Three columns: the **outline** of headings on the left, the pages in the middle, the **inspector** on the right. A chapter collapses and expands from the outline's context menu.
- With a keyboard, the **menu bar** has the Insert and Format menus, the review commands in the Edit menu, Print and Export As in the File menu, and in the View menu **Go to Page…**, **Collapse All Headings** and **Expand All Headings**, **Go Back** and **Go Forward**, Pageless and Focus Mode. The same shortcuts as on the Mac work; see [Keyboard shortcuts](/en/projects/documenta/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).
- On iPad you can open several Documenta windows.

## Scan, draw, handwrite

In the **+ › Objects** submenu, on iPhone and iPad only:

- **Scan Document…**: the camera crops and straightens the sheets; at the end you choose whether to put the recognized text at the cursor (**As Text**) or the pages (**As Images**).
- **Text from Image…**: the text of a photo picked from your library goes to the cursor.
- **Drawing…**: a canvas to draw on with Apple Pencil or your finger; the drawing goes on the page as an image, to move and resize.
- With Apple Pencil you handwrite into the text with Scribble.

Text recognition happens on the device; photos are picked with the system picker, without access to your library.

## Comments

**… › Review › Comments**, or tapping a comment mark in the margin, opens the comments panel, split into **Open** and **Resolved**. Each comment shows author, date, the commented text and the replies; tapping it takes you to the text. Swipe a comment right to resolve or reopen it, left to edit, reply to or delete it. At the bottom are **Previous Comment** and **Next Comment**, at the top **New Comment**.

## Settings

**… › Settings** is split into pages:

- **Writing**: autocorrection, smart quotes and dashes, links detected as you type, Paste and Match Style and **Format as You Type, Markdown Style**;
- **Spelling**: spell checking, language, checking each text in its own language and personal dictionary;
- **Substitutions**: replacements as you type, the same as on the Mac;
- **New Documents**: where to save them, format, paper, orientation, margins, page number and font;
- **Page and Units**: page appearance (light, dark or automatic, the default) and unit of measurement;
- **Reading**: reading speed, word goal, and voice and speed for reading aloud;
- at the bottom, the author name for comments and tracked changes and **Restore Default Settings**.

## Differences from the Mac

| Feature | Mac | iPhone and iPad |
|---|---|---|
| Opens and saves DOCX, ODT, RTF, Markdown, plain text | ✓ | ✓ |
| `.doc` files | opens and saves | opens in a new Word document |
| Headers and footers, page borders, watermarks, line numbers, hyphenation | ✓ | ✓ |
| Equations, WordArt, table formulas | ✓ | ✓ |
| Personal templates | documents you choose, wherever you like | files in the Templates folder |
| Focus Mode, dark page, word goal | ✓ | ✓ |
| Resolved comments, compare with an earlier version, clean copy | ✓ | ✓ |
| Collapsed headings, Go Back and Forward | ✓ | iPad |
| Ruler, copy and paste style, style editing, image reduction | ✓ | — |
| Scanning sheets, text from image, hand drawings | — | ✓ |
| Menu bar and shortcuts | ✓ | iPad with keyboard |
| iCloud Drive and Handoff | ✓ | ✓ |
