## Paragraph styles

Documenta has eleven paragraph styles: **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Heading 1** to **Heading 6**, **Body**, **Quote** and **Caption**. The first nine have a shortcut, from <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> (Title) to <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd> (Body).

A style's look can be changed for each document:

- **Format › Paragraph Style › Modify Style…**: font, size, bold, italic, color and spacing before and after. The new look applies to this document and to the paragraphs that already have the style.
- **Update Style from Selection**: the style takes the look of the paragraph the cursor is in.

When a style changes, paragraphs only change where they matched the old style: a word in italics stays in italics.

## Character styles

**Format › Character Style**: Emphasis, Strong, Code and Short Quote, or No Character Style. They are kept in Word and ODT files.

## Font

From the **Format › Font** menu and the Font section of the inspector:

- **Bold** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>), **Italic** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>), **Underline** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>U</kbd>), **Strikethrough** and **Double Strikethrough**.
- Single, double, thick, dotted, dashed or wavy underline, with its own color.
- **Small Caps** and **All Caps**: the text stays as typed and is shown in capitals. The case commands (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case) instead really change the text.
- **Superscript** (<kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>=</kbd>) and **Subscript** (<kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd>), **Bigger** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>+</kbd>) and **Smaller** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd>).
- Text color, highlight, letter spacing, **Effects** (letter outline and shadow).
- **Copy Style** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>) and **Paste Style** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd>) carry font and paragraph formatting from one place to another; **Clear Formatting** brings the text back to its paragraph style.

## Paragraph

- Alignment: left (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>{</kbd>), centered (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>|</kbd>), right (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>}</kbd>) or justified.
- Indents: <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>]</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>[</kbd>, or the values in the inspector and on the ruler.
- **Line Spacing** single, 1.15, 1.5 or double; spacing before and after the paragraph.
- **Move Paragraph Up** and **Down** (<kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd>) and **Sort Paragraphs** A to Z or Z to A.
- **Tabs**: left, center, right or decimal, with a dots, dashes or line leader. With no tab stops set, there is one every 1.27 cm. You can also drag them on the ruler.
- **Borders and Background**: ready-made boxes, a solid, double, dashed or dotted line for each side, color and shading. Consecutive paragraphs with the same box share a single box.
- **Drop Cap**: how many lines and characters, in the text or in the margin, and the distance from the text.

## Lists

**Bulleted List** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>8</kbd>) and **Numbered List** (<kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>7</kbd>); the inspector also has letters and Roman numerals, upper or lower case, the bullet symbol, the starting number and hierarchical numbering like 1.1. At the start of an item, <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> move it one level.

## Language of the text

**Format › Language** gives part of the text a language: spell checking uses that language's dictionary. **Use for Whole Document** applies it to all the text; the no-proofing option is for code, names and quotations. The language is kept in Word and ODT files.
