## What is rExif?

rExif is the metadata editor for photos and videos on your Mac. Wrong dates, missing locations, cryptic file names: fix them on one photo or thousands at once, and every change shows up in orange as a preview before it's written to your files. Nothing on disk changes until you click Save, and your photos never leave your Mac.

## Key Features

- **The right dates, even in bulk**: shift every date by an interval, for example when the camera clock was an hour behind, or correct it from one photo whose exact time you know
- **Dates from wherever they are**: from the file name (IMG_20230514_123456, WhatsApp, screenshots, Pixel…), the folder name, or Google Takeout JSON files; dates in sequence, missing ones filled in from their neighbors, time zone changes
- **File dates aligned**: file creation and modification dates matched to the capture date in one click
- **Location on the map**: drag photos onto the map to set their location, or search for a place by name
- **Automatic location**: from the photo closest in time, from a GPX track (Strava, Garmin…), or from Google Takeout; city, state, and country from the coordinates
- **Copies to share**: export copies without location or personal data
- **Text and keywords**: camera, lens, description, author, and copyright; templates with placeholders such as "© {year} Jane Smith", plus find and replace across every item; keywords and star ratings, also read by Lightroom
- **Batch rename**: with a template, for example `{date}_{nnn}`
- **Work fast**: a table with customizable columns, search, and quick selection (no date, no location, suspicious dates, by camera…); copy metadata from one photo and paste it onto others; export and import as CSV to fix it in a spreadsheet
- **Review before you save**: every change can be undone, redone, or reverted; Quick Look with the space bar
- **Every format**: JPEG, HEIC, PNG, and TIFF rewritten without recompressing the image; MOV, MP4, and M4V without re-encoding; RAW (CR2, NEF, ARW…) and AVI, MKV, MTS stay untouched, with metadata in an `.xmp` next to them, read by Lightroom, Bridge, and Premiere
- **Photos library**: change the date and location right in Photos, or export the originals
- **Built for macOS**: drag into the window or onto the Dock icon, "Open With…" in Finder, reopens your previous session
- **Light, dark, or automatic appearance**: interface in English and Italian
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

Available on **macOS** 15 Sequoia or later, for Macs with Apple silicon and Intel.
