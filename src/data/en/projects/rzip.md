## What is rZip?

rZip is the archive app for your Mac: look inside ZIP, RAR, 7z, and many other formats without extracting them, open password-protected or multi-part archives, and create your own with a simple drag.

## Key Features

- **Look inside, without extracting**: a complete list with expandable folders, sizes, dates, and file types; an archive summary with file count, space saved, and a chart by type
- **Previews in memory**: photos, PDFs, and text are shown with no temporary files
- **Search and browse**: search the whole archive, browse by category, go back and forward
- **Extract only what you need**: everything or just the selection, or drag a file straight to Finder; existing files are never overwritten
- **Your choice of destination**: ask every time, next to the archive, or a fixed folder
- **Protected and multi-part archives**: password-protected 7z, ZIP, and RAR, even with an encrypted file list; volumes (.001, .part1.rar, .z01…) opened from any part; integrity check without extracting
- **Create and edit**: 7z, ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, TAR.XZ, and TAR.BZ2 archives with a drag; AES-256 passwords, file names included, and split volumes; add or remove files in ZIP, 7z, and TAR without rebuilding them, with a backup copy in the Trash
- **No system clutter**: .DS_Store and __MACOSX are left out automatically
- **All the popular formats**: ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ISO, CAB, LHA, CPIO, XAR, plus JAR, EPUB, CBZ, and CBR
- **Native on macOS**: drop onto the Dock icon, full menus and keyboard shortcuts, light or dark appearance, interface in English and Italian
- **Privacy**: no account and no Internet connection, your files never leave your Mac
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
