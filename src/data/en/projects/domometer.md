## What is DomoMeter?

DomoMeter comes from a real problem: keeping track of household utility use means juggling paper bills, scraps of paper or spreadsheets that are hard to keep up to date. The app brings electricity, gas, water and any other meter together on one screen: you record your readings whenever you like and immediately see how much you use, how much you spend and where you will end up by the end of the period.

## Key Features

- **Meter readings** — for electricity, gas and water, with an editable date, notes, and meter replacements that don't distort your usage
- **Custom utilities** — up to ten utilities per home, each with its own name, icon, unit and colour
- **F1, F2 and F3 time bands** — on any utility, besides a single reading
- **Quick reading** — touch and hold a utility on the home screen (on iPhone you can also swipe right) to open the form already set to it
- **Reading calendar** — a year of readings at a glance; tap a day to see what you logged
- **Usage split across months** — even if you read the meter on irregular dates, monthly totals stay comparable
- **Forecasts and comparisons** — usage for the month, two months, six months or the year, an end-of-period forecast compared with the same period a year earlier, monthly and year-on-year charts
- **Yearly limits** — an expected usage for each utility, with the share already used
- **Tariffs and bills** — prices with history, per time band too, standing charge, an estimate of the current bill, budget and your choice of currency; estimated CO₂
- **Reading reminders** — for each utility, every month or every 2, 3, 6 or 12 months, on the day and at the time you choose
- **Multiple homes** — separate profiles for home, office or holiday home
- **Backup and CSV** — a full JSON backup that works across iPhone and Android; readings exported and imported as CSV
- **iOS only** — optional iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad
- **Android only** — Modern or Retro visual style, Material You dynamic colours, layouts for tablets and foldables
- **Privacy** — no account, no tracking; on Android the app does not connect to the Internet
- **No subscription** — free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

Available on **iPhone** and **iPad** with iOS/iPadOS 18 or later and on **Android** 8.0 or later, in seven languages.
