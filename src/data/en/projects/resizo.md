## What is Resizo?

Resizo is the fastest way to resize images on your Mac. Drop one photo or a hundred into the window, choose a size, and hit "Resize and Save". Resizo takes care of the rest. Everything happens right on your Mac, with no uploads and no account.

## Key Features

- **All at once**: resize whole folders of photos together, processed in parallel across your Mac's cores, with a progress bar and per-image results
- **Space saved**: see right away how much space you saved, photo by photo and in total
- **Precise sizes**: in pixels (width, height, or both, with locked proportions) or by percentage, from 5% to 300%
- **One-click presets**: Web (1920 px), Social (1080 px), Icon (512×512), Email (800 px), plus your own
- **Formats**: opens PNG, JPEG, HEIC, HEIF, TIFF, BMP, GIF, and WebP; saves in the original format, as PNG, or as JPEG with adjustable quality
- **HEIC to JPEG**: turn iPhone photos into files that work everywhere
- **Privacy first**: by default, strips EXIF metadata (date, camera, GPS location) from exported files
- **Originals stay safe**: always creates a new file with the suffix of your choice and never overwrites anything
- **Built for macOS**: drag onto the Dock icon, "Open With…" and the "Resize with Resizo" service in Finder, keyboard shortcuts
- **Light, dark, or automatic appearance**: interface in English and Italian
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

Available on **macOS** 13 Ventura or later (Apple Silicon and Intel).
