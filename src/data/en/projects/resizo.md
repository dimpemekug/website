## What is Resizo?

Resizo resizes images on your Mac, one or hundreds at a time. Drop photos or folders into the window, choose a size, and hit "Resize and Save": before saving you already see each image's new size and how much the files will weigh. Everything happens on your Mac, with no uploads and no account.

## Key Features

- **All at once**: whole folders of photos, processed in parallel on as many performance cores as your Mac has, with a progress bar, per-image results, and the option to cancel
- **Estimated size before saving**: the bottom of the options panel shows how much the files will weigh; when the batch is done you see the space saved, per photo and in total
- **Precise sizes**: in pixels (width, height, or both, with proportions locked or free) or by percentage from 5% to 300%, with 25, 50, 75, and 100% buttons; the ↑ ↓ arrows change sizes by 1 pixel, or by 10 with ⇧
- **Enlargement warning**: images that would come out larger than the original are marked in orange
- **One-click presets**: Web (1920 px), Social (1080 px), Icon (512×512), Email (800 px); save the current size as a preset, reorder presets, and choose which ones to show
- **Formats**: opens PNG, JPEG, HEIC, HEIF, TIFF, BMP, GIF, and WebP; saves in the original format, as PNG, or as JPEG with adjustable quality. Turns iPhone HEIC photos into JPEG
- **Privacy first**: by default, strips metadata (date, camera, GPS location) from exported files
- **Originals stay safe**: always creates a new file with the suffix of your choice and never overwrites anything, in Pictures › Resizo, in a folder you pick, or next to each original
- **Quick Look**: Space bar or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Y</kbd> on the selected images, just like in Finder
- **Batch finished in the background**: a badge on the Dock icon, a bounce, and a notification if you are using another app
- **Full settings**: what to do when a batch finishes, which options to start from, how many images to process at once, customizable keyboard shortcuts, allowed folders
- **Built for macOS**: drag onto the Dock icon, "Open With…" and the "Resize with Resizo" service in Finder
- **Light, dark, or automatic appearance**: interface in English and Italian
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
