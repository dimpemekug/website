## What is Horologe?

Horologe is the app for watch collectors who want to catalog their pieces in an organized and private way. Brand, reference, prices, photos, papers, services, accuracy and days on the wrist: all in one place, stored on your device and with no account.

## Key Features

- **Complete records** — brand, model, reference, serial number, movement, caliber, diameter, material, year, box and papers, plus custom fields
- **Photos and documents** — cover photo and gallery to browse full screen, with zoom; warranties, receipts and certificates attached as PDFs or images
- **Grid or list collection** — search, filters by status, brand and movement, ten sort orders and your recently worn watches
- **Status & wishlist** — owned, in repair, sold or on the wishlist, with the gain worked out when you sell
- **Prices in any currency** — euro, dollar, pound, franc, yen or another currency you type in, even a different one for each watch
- **Services and reminders** — service history with costs and a notification when a watch is due for service
- **Accuracy assistant** — two readings a day apart checked against your phone's clock, the result in seconds per day and a chart over time
- **Wear diary** — mark the watch you're wearing today and find your days in a monthly calendar
- **Statistics** — collection value and how it grew, brands and movements, upcoming services, sales, most worn pieces and cost per wear
- **Export & backup** — a PDF sheet for each watch, a PDF inventory for your insurer, a CSV of the collection and a ZIP backup with photos and attachments, compatible between iOS and Android; before importing or resetting, a safety copy you can restore
- **App lock** — Face ID, Touch ID or passcode on iOS; fingerprint, face or PIN on Android
- **Privacy** — no account and no servers: your data stays on your device (or in your private iCloud, if you turn on sync)
- **iOS only** — iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad, camera for photos, COSC tolerance band in the accuracy chart
- **Android only** — a custom graphic style with light or dark theme and eight accent colors or your wallpaper colors
- **No subscription** — free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

Available on **iPhone** and **iPad** with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and on **Android** 7.0 or later, including tablets and foldables, in seven languages: Italian, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Russian.
