## What is Legendum?

Legendum brings together your collection of manga, comics, books, and video: the files you own, your servers, and the catalogs you choose, in one library on iPhone and iPad.

Legendum does not host or distribute any content: it only reads what you add.

## Key Features

- **Your library**: CBZ, CBR, EPUB, PDF, FB2, Word, RTF, Markdown, and text files, or image folders, imported from the Files app; series, chapters, and covers organized automatically, with your progress always where you left it and the next chapter to read highlighted
- **Backups and undo**: back up your library and progress to a file, to keep or to bring to another device; a banner with "Undo" after marking chapters or removing a series
- **Your servers**: Komga, Kavita, Suwayomi, and Jellyfin, including on your home network, and any OPDS catalog, such as public-domain book catalogs
- **Extensions**: support for Aidoku and Paperback extensions and for video modules, from the repositories you add; "Migrate" moves the series of a source that stopped working to another one, with their progress
- **Comfortable reading**: single page, two-page spreads on iPad, or continuous scrolling for webtoons; right-to-left reading, zoom, and on-device image enhancement
- **Books**: EPUB, FB2, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT and Markdown in the text reader, with themes, highlights, bookmarks and search
- **Video**: the shows and films on your Jellyfin server, with seasons in order and watched episodes marked; a player with two engines (MKV too), ASS subtitles, Picture in Picture and intro and recap skipping, with progress going back to the server
- **Always with you**: downloads to read and watch offline, alerts for new chapters, optional iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad
- **Native on iOS 26**: sidebar on iPad, light or dark theme, in English and Italian
- **Privacy**: no account, no ads, no tracking
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

For **iPhone** and **iPad** with iOS/iPadOS 26 or later. At first, Legendum is available as a **beta on TestFlight**, Apple's app for trying preview versions.
