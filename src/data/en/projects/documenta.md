## What is Documenta?

Documenta is a native word processor for Mac. Write on pages laid out like paper, with margins, headers and notes, and open, edit and save the documents you receive in their own format: Word, OpenDocument, RTF or plain text. DOCX and ODT are read and written without a conversion step, so styles, tables, images and page setup stay in place.

## Key Features

- **Your formats**: Word documents (.docx and .doc), OpenDocument (.odt), RTF and plain text, opened and saved as they are; export to PDF, EPUB, web page and Markdown
- **Real pages**: margins and a ruler, portrait or landscape, one page below the other or two side by side; paper sizes from A3 to Tabloid, text columns, page borders, watermarks, line numbers and automatic hyphenation
- **Headers and footers**: in three parts, with page number and total, different on the first page and on even pages, typed right on the page with a double-click
- **Sections**: different paper, orientation, columns and headers in different parts of the document; columns can change mid-page
- **Long documents**: numbered headings, a table of contents and a table of figures with page numbers, numbered captions, cross-references, bookmarks, footnotes and endnotes
- **Comments and tracked changes**: margin comments, as in Word and LibreOffice; record your edits and accept or reject everyone else's
- **Outline in the sidebar**: click to jump to a heading, drag to move a whole chapter
- **Tables**: merged cells, per-cell borders and shading, ready-made styles, first row repeated on every page, sortable rows and formulas like `=SUM(ABOVE)`
- **Objects on the page**: text boxes, WordArt with curved text, shapes and images with text wrapping around them; rotate, group, align and distribute them
- **Complete formatting**: eleven paragraph styles you can redefine per document, character styles, copy and paste style, multilevel lists, dot-leader tabs, drop caps, paragraph shading and borders
- **Writing tools**: find and replace by formatting too and with regular expressions, spell checking in the language of each part of the text, a personal dictionary, read aloud, a word goal, reading time and a readability score
- **Start from a template**: ten ready-made templates in the welcome window (letter, report, thesis, résumé…) and your own documents saved as templates
- **Focus Mode and dark page**: only the paragraph you're writing stays highlighted; the dark page applies on screen only
- **Private**: no account, no Internet connection, your documents never leave your Mac; interface in English and Italian
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

## Platforms

Available for **macOS** 15 Sequoia or later, on Macs with Apple silicon and Intel.
