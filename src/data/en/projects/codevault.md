## What is CodeVault?

CodeVault is your personal archive for code snippets and technical notes, built for developers who want to find what they already wrote in seconds. Handy one-liners, configs, terminal commands, or a project's documentation: everything stays organized, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Key Features

- **Snippets in 20 languages**: Swift, SwiftUI, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Kotlin, Java, C, C++, C#, PHP, SQL, Bash, HTML, CSS, JSON, YAML, and more, with syntax highlighting, line numbers, wrapping for long lines, and six editor themes
- **Several code blocks in one snippet**: each with its own title and its own Copy button
- **Markdown notes**: Markdown colors as you type, Return continues lists, checkboxes, tables, footnotes, and pasting from Safari, Mail, or Pages turns formatted text into Markdown
- **Markdown bar above the keyboard** on iPhone and iPad; on Mac the same commands sit above the editor
- **Links between notes**: type `[[Title]]` to open another note; at the bottom of each note, "Linked From" lists the notes that point to it
- **Preview and PDF export**: callouts, a table of contents, checkboxes you tick in the preview, word count, and reading time
- **Find everything instantly**: categories, color-coded tags, pinned items, and search across titles, code, notes, categories, and tags, with filters such as `lang:swift` or `tag:cache`
- **Home in three views**: one line of code, a compact list, or a third view of each style's own (file list, numbered index, or tiles)
- **Three styles for the whole app**: Terminal, Typographic, and Glass, with a light, dark, or automatic theme
- **iCloud sync**: automatic across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with a local-only fallback if iCloud isn't available
- **Face ID or Touch ID lock**: optional, to protect access to the app
- **Restorable trash**: deleted items can be recovered for 30 days
- **Backup and export**: JSON backup and import, Markdown export, sharing from the system share sheet
- **User guide and sample data** in Settings, to try everything straight away
- **Native on every platform**: sidebar and multi-column layout on Mac and iPad, compact navigation on iPhone, keyboard shortcuts on Mac
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
- **Available in 7 languages**: English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Russian

## Platforms

Available on **iPhone** and **iPad** with iOS/iPadOS 18 or later and on **Mac** with macOS 15 Sequoia or later: one free app for all your devices.
