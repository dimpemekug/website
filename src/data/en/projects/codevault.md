## What is CodeVault?

CodeVault is your personal archive for code snippets and technical notes, built for developers who want to find what they already wrote in seconds. Handy one-liners, configs, terminal commands, or project notes: everything stays organized, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Key Features

- **Snippets in 20 languages**: Swift, SwiftUI, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Kotlin, Java, C, C++, C#, PHP, SQL, Bash, HTML, CSS, JSON, YAML, and more, with syntax highlighting and line numbers
- **Plain-text or Markdown notes**: for documentation, checklists, and technical write-ups
- **Find everything instantly**: custom categories, color-coded tags, pinned items, and full-text search across titles, content, and categories
- **Home at a glance**: your recent items and most-used categories, always up front
- **iCloud sync**: automatic across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with a local-only fallback if iCloud isn't available
- **Face ID or Touch ID lock**: optional, to protect access to the app
- **Restorable trash**: deleted items can be recovered for 30 days
- **Export and share**: backup and export to JSON, share as Markdown, export notes to PDF
- **Native on every platform**: sidebar and multi-column layout on Mac and iPad, compact navigation on iPhone, keyboard shortcuts on Mac
- **No subscription**: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
- **Available in 7 languages**: English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Russian

## Platforms

Available on **iOS** and **iPadOS** 18 or later and on **macOS** 15 Sequoia or later: one free app for all your devices.
