*Updated November 6, 2026*

Documenta is an app for Mac, iPhone and iPad that does not collect, store or transmit any personal
data.

## No data collection

- The app does not connect to any server, ours or anyone else's. On the Mac, the macOS sandbox
  prevents any Internet connection.
- There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- The documents you open, edit and save stay on your device, in the Documenta folder of your
  iCloud Drive, or in the cloud service you chose yourself. iCloud Drive and Handoff are system
  services run by Apple with your account: we never see your documents.

## Camera and photos (iPhone and iPad)

- The camera is used only to scan paper sheets when you choose Insert › Scan Document; the app
  asks for permission the first time.
- Photos are picked with the system picker: the app receives only the ones you choose, without
  access to your library.
- Text in scanned pages and photos is recognized on the device. Text and images end up only in the
  document you insert them into.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Preferences** (zoom, ruler, inspector, word count, spell checking, personal text
  replacements, the author name for comments and tracked changes) in the app settings.
- **Personal dictionary**: the words you add, in the app settings on the Mac; they are also
  taught to the system spell checker, which keeps them on your device.
- **Personal templates** (Mac): the documents you save as a template, in the app's Application
  Support folder.
- **Performance reports** (Mac): through MetricKit, macOS gives the app anonymous reports on launch
  times, hangs and crashes. The app keeps them for about a month in its Application Support folder
  and does not send them to anyone.
- **Recent documents**: managed by the system (File menu on the Mac, the Files app on iPhone and
  iPad).

None of this data is read by us or sent anywhere.
