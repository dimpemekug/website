*Updated October 24, 2026*

Documenta is a Mac app that does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## No data collection

- The app has no network access: the macOS sandbox prevents any Internet connection.
- There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- The documents you open, edit and save stay on your Mac (or in the cloud service you chose yourself, such as iCloud Drive, managed by the system).

## Data that stays on your Mac

- **Preferences** (zoom, ruler, inspector, word count) in the app settings.
- **Recent documents**: managed by macOS in the File menu.

None of this data is read by us or sent anywhere.
